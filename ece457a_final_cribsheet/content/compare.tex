\section{Metaheuristic Comparison}
\subsection*{Explore vs Exploit Control}
\resizebox{\linewidth}{!}{%
\(
\begin{array}{lll}
\text{Method} & \text{Explore knob} & \text{Exploit knob}\\
\hline
\text{SA} & \text{high }T & \text{low }T\\
\text{Tabu} & \text{freq.\ memory} & \text{best-move pick}\\
\text{ACO} & \text{evaporation }\rho,\beta & \text{pheromone }\alpha\\
\text{PSO} & \text{inertia }w & \text{cog.+soc.\ pull}\\
\text{GA} & \text{mutation, }p_c & \text{selection, elitism}\\
\text{DE} & \text{scale }F & \text{greedy select}\\
\text{ES} & \text{large }\sigma & \text{small }\sigma\\
\text{Memetic} & \text{EA operators} & \text{local search}
\end{array}
\)
}\\
Population methods (GA, ACO, PSO, DE, ES): many candidates, built-in diversity, escape local optima. Trajectory methods (HC, SA, Tabu): one state, cheap, need memory/randomness to escape.

Minimisation: exploitation descends within a basin (local opt); exploration hops over the hill to the global opt. High \(T\)/mutation/inertia widen the hops; greedy/local moves deepen the current basin.

\runsub{Which Algorithm When.}
Continuous, smooth, differentiable: local search / gradient beats metaheuristics (No Free Lunch: no method wins everywhere).\\
Combinatorial routing / assignment / TSP / cell placement: ACO (incremental construction over a graph) or GA with permutation encoding.\\
Continuous multimodal function optimisation: PSO or DE (both move by population geometry).\\
Symbolic model / equation discovery from data: GP (evolves executable structure).\\
Hard structure + smooth parameters: memetic (GP or GA for structure, local search for parameters).\\
Real-vector optimisation with self-tuned steps: ES with \(1/5\) rule or covariance adaptation.

\runsub{GP Loop (pseudocode).}
\texttt{init random pop of trees}\\
\texttt{while not done:}\\
\quad\texttt{fitness[i] = error(tree[i], data)}\\
\quad\texttt{parents = select(pop, fitness)}\\
\quad\texttt{child = subtree\_crossover(p1,p2)}\\
\quad\texttt{child = subtree\_mutation(child)}\\
\quad\texttt{pop = replace(pop, child)}\\
\texttt{return best tree}\\
Building blocks = useful subtrees; closure keeps every generated tree evaluable.

\subsection*{Extra Formulas \& Reminders}
SA cooling: geometric \(T_{k+1}=\alpha T_k\) (\(0.8\le\alpha<1\)), linear \(T_k=T_0-k\beta\), logarithmic \(T_k=T_0/\log(1+k)\) (only log guarantees asymptotic global convergence, but is impractically slow).\\
GA selection: roulette \(p_i=f_i/\sum_j f_j\) (scale-sensitive, weak late); tournament = best of \(k\) random (pressure rises with \(k\), needs no scaling); rank-based uses order only (scale/translation invariant); SUS = evenly spaced pointers; elitism keeps best \(E\).\\
All metaheuristics are anytime: termination (max generations/evaluations, convergence, or hitting a target) never implies optimality. UCS and A* goal-test on expansion, not generation. Alpha--beta returns exactly the minimax value. Greedy, beam, and hill climbing are neither complete nor optimal.
