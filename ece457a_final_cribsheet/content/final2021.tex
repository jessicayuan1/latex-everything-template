\runsub{Q1 search + crossover.}
\textbf{(b)} Asks: which algorithm best manages worst-case time/space, and how? Iterative Deepening --- DFS-like linear memory (no stored frontier) yet never builds paths longer than the shortest solution.\\
\textbf{(c)} Asks: reorder words of an unknown language using a ``ridiculousness'' score --- search-space size?; define a neighbourhood + 2 examples; will HC / Tabu always find a valid sentence? Space \(=n!\); neighbourhood = swap two words' positions. HC no (local optima); Tabu no either (only optimal as \(t\to\infty\)).\\
\textbf{(d)} Asks: first offspring by Cycle Crossover and by PMX (cuts after locus 2,6). PMX \(\Rightarrow425318976\); CX excluded (not on our final).\\
\textbf{(e)} Asks: two parents differ in every bit; 1-point crossover at random \(c\in[1,n]\) --- P(children are clones)? \(=1/n\) (only when the cut is at an extreme end).\\
\textbf{(f)} Asks: when is exploration vs exploitation more desirable? Exploit when good guesses exist (seed a GA); explore when none exist (use ES).

\runsub{Q2 MDP treasure ($\gamma{=}1$).}

Asks: \(V^*(S_9),V^*(S_{13}),V^*(S_\infty)\) and the min state-space size. \(V^*(S_9)=20\); \(V^*(S_{13})=\tfrac{40+30}{2}=35\) (peek, else fall back); \(V^*(S_\infty)=10{+}20{+}80+\tfrac{40}{2}+\tfrac{30}{2}+\tfrac{160}{4}=185\); size \(=MN\cdot3^{B}\cdot2^{X}\) (position \(\times\) boulder present/absent/unknown \(\times\) treasure taken/not).

\runsub{Q6 EA/ES/ACO.}
\textbf{(a)} Asks: EA mutation vs PSO velocity update difference. Mutation = random perturbation; velocity is directed by \(pbest/gbest\). \textbf{(b)} Asks: how ES adapts. Self-adaptation: \(\sigma\) encoded in the individual, evolves with it. \textbf{(c)} ACO TSP (ant at C, visited A, \(\alpha{=}\beta{=}1\), \(\eta{=}1/d\)): asks P to A/B/D, update counts, density vs quantity, \(\alpha{=}0\)/\(\beta{=}0\), variant names. \(P(A){=}0\); \(P(B){=}\tfrac{0.98/6}{0.98/6+0.67/3}{=}0.422\); \(P(D){=}0.578\). Updates/iter (5 ants): step-by-step 15, delayed 5, offline 1. Density adds \(Q\) (ignores length); quantity adds \(Q/d_{ij}\). \(\alpha{=}0\Rightarrow\)greedy heuristic; \(\beta{=}0\Rightarrow\)pure pheromone. Variants: adaptive per-ant=Near-Param-Free ACS; bounded range=MMAS; GA-tuned=ACSGA; pseudo-random/elitist=ACS; delayed-all=AS.
