\section{Uninformed (Blind) Search}

\subsection*{Generic Search Loop}
Build search tree over problem graph; repeat: choose a node from fringe, goal-test, expand (generate children).\\
Strategy = how the next fringe node is chosen (the data structure).\\
Node states: explored / on frontier (fringe) / unexplored.\\
Repeated states: keep lowest-cost; check on generation (adding to fringe) or on expansion (popping).

\subsection*{BFS}
Expand shallowest node; fringe = FIFO queue; new successors to the back.\\
Nodes generated to depth \(d\): \( 1+b+b^2+\cdots+b^d=O(b^{d+1}) \).\\
Complete (if \(b\) finite); optimal only if path cost = depth (equal step costs); returns shallowest goal.\\
Time \( O(b^{d+1}) \), Space \( O(b^{d+1}) \).

\subsection*{Uniform-Cost Search (UCS)}
Expand cheapest node by path cost \( g(n) \); fringe = priority queue.\\
Goal test ON EXPANSION (not generation): a generated goal may be costlier than a queued node, optimal only when it reaches the front.\\
Needs step cost \( \ge \varepsilon>0 \) (no zero/negative edges). \(=\) Dijkstra; \(=\) BFS when costs equal.\\
Complete + optimal. Effective depth \(\lfloor C^*/\varepsilon\rfloor\).\\
Time/Space \(O\!\left(b^{\,1+\lfloor C^*/\varepsilon\rfloor}\right)\); \(C^*\)=optimal cost.

\subsection*{DFS}
Expand deepest node; fringe = LIFO stack.\\
NOT complete (infinite depth / loops); NOT optimal.\\
Time \( O(b^m) \); Space \( O(b\,m) \) (linear) -- main advantage.\\
Can beat BFS for dense solution spaces.

\subsection*{Depth-Limited Search (DLS)}
DFS with cutoff \(l\); always terminates.\\
Complete iff \( d\le l \) (fails if \(l<d\)); not optimal.\\
Time \( O(b^l) \), Space \( O(b\,l) \). If \( l\gg d \), costlier than needed.

\subsection*{Iterative Deepening (IDS/DFID)}
Run DLS with \( l=0,1,2,\dots \) Combines DFS space with BFS completeness.\\
Complete (if \(b\) finite); optimal if path cost non-decreasing in depth.\\
Time \( O(b^d) \), Space \( O(b\,d) \). Re-expansion overhead is small (lowest levels dominate).

\subsection*{Bidirectional}
Search forward from \(s_0\) and backward from goal; meet in middle.\\
Time/Space \( \approx O(b^{d/2}) \); needs invertible actions + known goal state.

\subsection*{Comparison}
\resizebox{\linewidth}{!}{%
\(
\begin{array}{lccccc}
 & \text{Compl.} & \text{Opt.} & \text{Time} & \text{Space} \\
\hline
\text{BFS} & Y^{b} & Y^{c} & b^{d+1} & b^{d+1}\\
\text{UCS} & Y & Y & b^{1+C^*/\varepsilon} & b^{1+C^*/\varepsilon}\\
\text{DFS} & N & N & b^{m} & b\,m\\
\text{DLS} & Y^{l\ge d} & N & b^{l} & b\,l\\
\text{IDS} & Y^{b} & Y^{c} & b^{d} & b\,d\\
\text{Bidir} & Y^{b} & Y^{c} & b^{d/2} & b^{d/2}
\end{array}
\)
}\\
\(b\): \(b\) finite. \(c\): equal/non-decreasing step costs.
