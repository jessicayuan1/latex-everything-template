\section{Trajectory Metaheuristics}

A trajectory method = (i) representation \(s\), (ii) neighbourhood \(N(s)\), (iii) acceptance/memory control. The move operator decides which local optima are reachable.

\subsection*{Hill Climbing}
Move to best neighbour: \( s_{k+1}\in\arg\min_{s'\in N(s_k)} E(s') \) (descent) until no improvement.\\
Variants: simple/first-choice (take first improving), steepest-ascent (best neighbour), stochastic, random-restart.\\
Fails on: local optima, plateaus (flat), ridges. Fixes: restarts, sideways moves, larger \(N\).\\
NOT complete, NOT optimal; Time \( O(\infty) \), Space \( O(b) \).

\subsection*{Simulated Annealing}
Accept neighbour \(s'\) with \( \Delta E=E(s')-E(s) \):\\
\(
P=\begin{cases}1 & \Delta E\le 0\\ e^{-\Delta E/T} & \Delta E>0\end{cases}
\)\\
(minimisation; flip sign of \(\Delta E\) for maximisation). Draw \( u\sim U(0,1) \), accept if \( u\le P \).\\
High \(T\): explore (accept many worse moves). Low \(T\): exploit (\(\to\) hill climbing).\\
Cooling: geometric \( T\leftarrow\alpha T \) (\(0{<}\alpha{<}1\)), linear, or logarithmic. Reheating escapes stagnation.\\
Analogy: cooling metal to a low-energy crystal. Markov-chain convergence to global opt with slow enough schedule.

\subsection*{SA Acceptance Example}
\( e^{-0.2}\approx 0.818 \).\\
For \( \Delta E=2 \): \(T{=}5\!:e^{-0.4}{=}0.67\); \(T{=}1\!:e^{-2}{=}0.135\); \(T{=}0.1\!:e^{-20}\!\approx\!0\).\\
Lower \(T\) \(\Rightarrow\) worse moves rarely accepted.\\
Accept/reject (min), \(E(s){=}10,\,E(s'){=}13,\,\Delta E{=}3\):\\
\(T{=}10\!:P=e^{-0.3}=0.74\), draw \(u{=}0.5\le0.74\Rightarrow\) accept.\\
\(T{=}1\!:P=e^{-3}=0.05\), draw \(u{=}0.5>0.05\Rightarrow\) reject.

\subsection*{Tabu Search (TS)}
Deterministic; escapes minima via memory not randomness.\\
Candidate set \( C(s)=(N(s)\setminus T)\cup A(s) \): non-tabu neighbours plus aspirated moves.\\
Each step: evaluate \(N(s)\), pick best admissible move (even if worse), update tabu list + best-so-far.\\
Tabu (short-term/recency): forbids recently used move attributes for a tabu tenure (e.g. swap matrix \( \text{Tabu}(i,j)\leftarrow T \), decrement each iteration; tabu while \(>0\)).\\
Aspiration: override tabu if move beats best-so-far \( E(s')<E(s^*) \) (permission, not forced selection).\\
Long-term/frequency memory: penalise often-used attributes \( \text{Score}=\text{Value}+F(i,j) \) \(\Rightarrow\) diversification. Recency-based intensification fixes persistent components.\\
Tabu prevents cycling, not progress. Tenure too short \(\Rightarrow\) cycles; too long \(\Rightarrow\) over-restriction (adapt between \(L_{min},L_{max}\)).

\subsection*{TS Tiny-TSP Example (2-opt)}
\( s_0{=}A\text{-}B\text{-}D\text{-}C\text{-}E\text{-}A,\;E{=}25 \). Move: cut \((B,D),(C,E)\), add \((B,C),(D,E)\):\\
\( E(s_1)=25-(6{+}4)+(2{+}2)=19 \); Tabu \(\leftarrow\{(B,C),(D,E)\}\), tenure \(L{=}2\).\\
Next iter: reverse move removes tabu edges \(\Rightarrow\) blocked; best admissible \(\to E=23\) (worse, but avoids cycling).

\subsection*{TS Aspiration Example}
\(E(s_k){=}20\), best \(E(s^*){=}19\). Candidates: \(m_1\)(tabu,17), \(m_3\)(18), \(m_4\)(21).\\
\(m_1\) is tabu but \(17<19\Rightarrow\) aspiration accepts \(m_1\).
